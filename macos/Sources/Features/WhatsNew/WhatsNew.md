# Updates tell you what's new

When a new Lostty is ready, your open windows offer Restart to Update, and
it keeps every terminal. After the update, this card tells you what changed,
once.

- A banner on your open windows when an update is ready, with Restart to Update.
- The next window you open shows what's in the update, before you restart.
- Lostty Settings → Updates: install updates automatically, or ask first.
