Your phone talks to your Mac. Nothing else does.

          
The phone reaches your Mac directly, over SSH, inside your own private Tailscale network. There's no Lostty server in the middle. There isn't one.

        

      

      

        

          

            
          

        

        

          
Direct, inside your tailnet
Install Tailscale on the Mac and the iPhone and sign in to both with the same account. The phone connects straight to your Mac.

          
The key never leaves the phone
Pairing adds the phone's public key to your Mac. Lose the phone and you click Remove next to it in Phone Access.

          
Reachable with the lid closed
Stay Awake keeps the Mac working with the lid shut, even on battery. If its Wi-Fi drops, it hops onto your iPhone's hotspot.

        

      

    


    
    

      

        

          

            
Built on Ghostty.

            
Lostty started from Ghostty, the fast, native terminal by Mitchell Hashimoto and the Ghostty contributors. Every character you see is drawn by Ghostty's engine, which does the hard part quickly and correctly. Lostty adds the Mac and iPhone side on top.

            
If you love how fast Lostty feels, that's Ghostty. Go give it a star.

Getting Started

          
Lostty runs on your Mac. The iPhone app connects to it over your own Tailscale network. You'll build both from source, so most of the setup is waiting for the first build.


          
What you need

          
    
            
  • A Mac on macOS 15 or later
    • 
            
  • An iPhone on iOS 17 or later, and its cable
    • 
            
  • A free Apple Account
    • 
          


          
On your Mac

          
    
            
  1. Install Xcode 26
    Get it from the App Store and open it once. You'll also need Homebrew.
    2. 
            
  2. Install Lostty
    In Terminal:
    
              
    zsh
    

    git clone https://github.com/Lawliang/lostty.git
cd lostty
macos/setup.sh
    
              
    It installs what the build needs (it may ask for your password), then builds Lostty, puts it in /Applications and opens it.
    
            
    3. 
          

          
Updating later
Run git pull, then macos/install.sh, then choose Restart to Update in Lostty's menu bar. Don't Quit Lostty to update: quitting ends every terminal session.


          
On your iPhone

          
    
            
  1. Add your Apple Account to Xcode
    In Xcode, open Settings → Accounts, click + and sign in.
    2. 
            
  2. Install the app
    Plug your iPhone into the Mac, unlock it and tap Trust. Then, in the lostty folder:
    
              
    zsh
    

    macos/install-phone.sh
    
              
    The first time, the phone may ask you to turn on Settings → Privacy & Security → Developer Mode (the phone restarts) and to trust your Apple Account in Settings → General → VPN & Device Management. Then run the command again.
    
            
    3. 
          

          
With a free Apple Account, the app stops opening after 7 days.
Run macos/install-phone.sh again to renew it. A paid developer account makes it last a year.


          
Pair them

          
    
            
  1. Install Tailscale on both
    Install Tailscale on the Mac and the iPhone, and sign in to both with the same account.
    2. 
            
  2. Pair
    On the Mac, open Lostty → Phone Access… and fix any row without a ✓. Each row says what to click. Then click Pair a Phone…. On the iPhone, open Lostty, tap Pair with Mac and scan the code. Click Allow on the Mac.
    3. 
          

          
Why Tailscale?
Your phone talks to your Mac directly, over SSH, inside your own private Tailscale network. There's no Lostty server in the middle, and the phone's SSH key never leaves the phone.


          
Go take a walk

          
Your phone reaches the Mac while it's awake and Lostty is open. To keep it reachable with the lid closed, install the Stay-awake permission in Phone Access, then choose Stay Awake for Phone from Lostty's menu bar icon.

          

        


        

          
Phone Access

          
Open your Mac's Lostty sessions from your iPhone, anywhere, over your own Tailscale. Nothing goes through a Lostty server; there isn't one.

          
Set up once

          
On the Mac, open Lostty → Phone Access… and fix any row that isn't ✓:

          
    
            
  • tmux: brew install tmux.
    • 
            
  • Remote Login and Password logins off: one admin password prompt turns on Remote Login and allows only key logins.
    • 
            
  • Terminal definition: click Install.
    • 
            
  • Folder access (optional): click Grant Now so commands from your phone never wait on a macOS prompt.
    • 
          

          
Using it

          
Lostty on your phone lists your Mac's sessions and keeps the list current while it's open. A Claude Code session shows its task, with Waiting when it's waiting for you or Working while it works. Waiting sessions come first. Opening one on the phone counts as seeing it, on the Mac too.

          
Swipe left on a row and choose Rename to name a session. It's the same name as Rename Tab… on the Mac.

          
Lost phone

          
Open Lostty → Phone Access on the Mac and click Remove next to it.

          

        


        

          
Skins

          
Lostty gives every pane a background color and texture based on the project it's in. cd into a project and the pane re-skins; leave and it reverts.

          
Presets

          
Twelve built-in skins, each with a rarity and an accent color. The arcade pack: neon-arcade, sunset-drive, mint-protocol, deep-dive, lava-rush and bubble-pop. The Olympian boon pack, each offered by a patron god: thunderhead, undertow, bloodrite, heartsease, silverbow and stormsurge.

          
Change a pane's skin

          
zsh


skins set neon-arcade
skins texture grid
skins reset

          
Claude Trace

          
While Claude Code is working in a pane, a light ray races around that pane's edge. The moment Claude finishes, or asks for permission, the ray disappears. With Reduce Motion on, the border glows gently instead.

          

        


        

          
Manual Mac setup

          
Most people should use Phone Access. These are the steps it automates, for setting up by hand.

          
SSH and tmux

          
    
            
  1. brew install tmux
    2. 
            
  2. System Settings → General → Sharing → Remote Login on, allowed for only your user.
    3. 
            
  3. Keys only: put PasswordAuthentication no and KbdInteractiveAuthentication no in /etc/ssh/sshd_config.d/000-lostty.conf, then toggle Remote Login off and on.
    4. 
          

          
The phone's key

          
In the phone app, open Settings → Show This Phone's Key and append it to ~/.ssh/authorized_keys as restrict,pty <key> lostty-phone:<id>.

          
Awake in a bag

          
Optional: sudo pmset -a disablesleep 1, and set the Mac's Wi-Fi to join the iPhone's hotspot automatically. Expect heat under heavy builds.

          

        


        

          
Contributing

          
Lostty is MIT licensed and still merges from upstream Ghostty, so engine and other-platform code stays in the tree.

          
Build

          
zsh


zig build -Demit-macos-app=false
macos/build.nu

          
Test

          
    
            
  • Engine: zig build test -Dtest-filter=<name>
    • 
            
  • Mac app: macos/build.nu --action test
    • 
            
  • Phone package: cd macos/LosttyRemote && swift test
