Lostty tab icons_

Five replacements for the axolotl favicon. Each is drawn from simple shapes, not text, so it stays sharp at the 16px a browser tab gives it. Prompt, magenta is the one lostty.org uses.

Light browser
Dark browser

Whichever you pick goes in as favicon.svg (built by website/build.mjs), plus a 180px PNG for iPhone home screens. The axolotl stays on the page itself.
